What actually moves

Navigation goes down the side, with counts
Five views in a pill row tell you nothing until you open each one. In the rail they carry a number, so three overdue to-dos are visible from the board. The number turns amber only when something is late.
The range switch joins the heading
Right now views and range are two stacked rows that look alike and do different jobs. One row of furniture instead of two, and Day/Week/Month sits on the view it controls.
Undated work gets a tray at the top
Anything with no date is invisible on a board organised by day, so it currently hides inside the to-do list. The tray puts it where you will see it and lets you drag it onto a day.
One line of plain state under the heading
How many blocks, how many unwritten, and what you can do here — instead of working it out by counting rows.
Phone gets a bottom bar
Thumb reach, and the badge rides the tab, so you do not scroll a tab strip to find out something is waiting.

Nothing is wired up. This page is furniture only — the blocks above are made up, the buttons do not navigate, and your working dashboard is untouched at its own link. Flip between Now and Proposed to compare; the board below the chrome is deliberately identical in both so only the shell changes.
